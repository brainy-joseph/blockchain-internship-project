The Problem

Traditional ticketing has three massive leaks that cost the industry ~$27 billion every year.

Scalping & Price Gouging

Bots buy 60%+ of limited tickets and resell at 5–50x face value. FIFA World Cup final tickets hit $20,000+ on StubHub vs. a $1,600 face value. Fans are priced out.

Fraud & Counterfeits

Fake Pokemon "1st Edition" cards flood eBay. Counterfeit FIFA tickets leave fans stranded at stadium gates. Chargebacks cost the industry ~$4 billion/year.

Zero Creator Royalties

Nintendo, FIFA, and artists receive $0 from secondary resales. An estimated ~$8 billion in potential royalty revenue is lost annually to scalpers and middlemen.

The Solution

We tokenize tickets as programmable NFTs on Ethereum. The smart contract enforces rules that no human or company can bypass.

  • 🛡️

    Price Ceiling Enforcement

    The smart contract hard-codes a max resale price (e.g., 120% of face value). Any transaction above the cap is automatically rejected. No scalper can bypass this not even the event organizer.

  • 🔒

    Soulbound Transfer Windows

    Tickets are "soulbound" (non-transferable) until a set time before the event, e.g., 48–72 hours. This stops early bot accumulation and forces fair primary sales.

  • 📜

    On-Chain Provenance

    Every owner, price, and transaction is recorded permanently on the blockchain. Buyers can verify authenticity instantly thus, no more fake Pokemon cards or counterfeit FIFA tickets.

  • 💰

    Programmable Royalty Splits

    On every resale, the smart contract auto-distributes revenue: 5% to FIFA, 3% to the host nation, 2% to a fan fund. Creators finally earn from the value they create.

Tech Stack

Blockchain

Ethereum (Sepolia testnet for demo). ERC-721 standard with custom anti-scalping logic.

Smart Contract

Solidity + OpenZeppelin. Price ceilings, transfer locks, and royalty distribution enforced on-chain.

Frontend

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS + Ethers.js. No build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages.

L2 Scaling

Polygon or Arbitrum for production. Reduces gas fees from $5 to under $0.01 per ticket.
