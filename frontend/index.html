How to Demo
1. You're already connected as Organizer. Click Mint Ticket to create a FIFA or Pokemon ticket for Fan A.
2. Switch to Fan A wallet. Try listing at 0.1 ETH (above the 0.06 cap). BLOCKED by smart contract!
3. Enable Demo Override to bypass the 5-minute soulbound lock (demo only).
4. Now list it at 0.055 ETH (within cap). It appears in the Marketplace.
5. Switch to Fan B wallet. Buy the ticket from the marketplace.
6. Switch back to Organizer and redeem the ticket to simulate gate scanning.
7. Check Fan B's wallet, the ticket now shows REDEEMED.
About Protocol
This is a browser-simulated demo of an NFT Ticketing & Anti-Scalping Protocol on Ethereum.
In production, the same logic runs as a Solidity smart contract deployed on Ethereum (or Polygon L2).
The anti-scalping rules price ceilings and transfer windows are enforced on-chain and cannot be bypassed by anyone.
